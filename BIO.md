# Bio, for use everywhere

Use these word for word on profiles (Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Google, LinkedIn featured, speaker intros) so search engines and AI tools see one consistent story. Seller first, buyers still served.

## One line
Bisendra Melaram, Team Leader of Keller Williams Greater Nassau in Garden City, NY, and a seller's agent on Long Island's South Shore. Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003.

## Short (about 80 words)
Bisendra Melaram is the Team Leader of Keller Williams Greater Nassau in Garden City, NY, and a seller's agent on Long Island's South Shore. He runs the office like a high-performing real estate team, and in 2025 it closed 545 transactions totaling more than $425 million in sales volume. A Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003, he holds the Seller Representative Specialist (SRS) designation and the Pricing Strategy Advisor (PSA) certification, along with SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD and RSPS.

## Long (about 232 words)
Bisendra Melaram is the Team Leader of Keller Williams Greater Nassau in Garden City, where he runs the brokerage like a high-performing real estate team with himself at the helm. In 2025 the office closed 545 transactions representing more than $425 million in sales volume, and in the first quarter of 2026 its agents closed 111 units totaling $81.8 million. He works with agents and leadership teams to turn strategy into measurable production and stronger client service.

He is also a seller's agent on Long Island's South Shore and a Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003, with more than 23 years in New York real estate. His career runs through complex markets, distressed properties, shifting buyer expectations and fast changes in technology. Earlier roles in systems integration and facilities management shaped how he approaches process and technology today.

For sellers, that means a price built on real closed sales, a house prepared for the questions buyers ask now, and clear steps from listing to closing. He also represents buyers, often clients who are selling one home and buying the next. He holds the SRS, SRES and ABR designations and the PSA, SFR, MRP, AHWD and RSPS certifications and has long ties to the South Asian community.

He writes The Key with Bisendra, a weekly newsletter, and speaks about real estate strategy, team performance and the practical use of AI in real estate.

## Credentials, full names
Designations: Seller Representative Specialist (SRS), Seniors Real Estate Specialist (SRES), Accredited Buyer's Representative (ABR).
Certifications: Pricing Strategy Advisor (PSA), Short Sales and Foreclosure Resource (SFR), Military Relocation Professional (MRP), At Home With Diversity (AHWD), Resort and Second-Home Property Specialist (RSPS).
Names and categories follow the National Association of REALTORS acronym guide. RSPS is NAR's acronym; use it in place of RSSP.

## Left out on purpose
- "CEO" and "CAIO". New York limits the titles licensees can use in advertising. Add a title only after your broker confirms it.

## Rules for the office numbers
- Always the office's totals ("the office closed", "its agents closed"), never "he sold".
- Source: your LinkedIn About section, 2025 and Q1 2026. Update them when the office reports new numbers.
- "Team Leader" always appears with your license type, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson.
- "First-time buyer specialist". Not how you want to be known.
