# Bio, for use everywhere

Use these word for word on profiles (Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Google, LinkedIn featured, speaker intros) so search engines and AI tools see one consistent story.

## One line
Bisendra Melaram, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson with Keller Williams Greater Nassau in Garden City, NY, licensed since 2003 and holder of the SRES designation.

## Short (about 50 words)
Bisendra Melaram is a Licensed Real Estate Salesperson based in Garden City, New York, with more than 23 years in New York real estate. He leads Keller Williams Greater Nassau, holds the Seniors Real Estate Specialist (SRES) designation, and helps buyers and sellers across Long Island's South Shore. He writes and speaks about real estate strategy and AI.

## Long (about 150 words)
Bisendra Melaram has worked in New York real estate for more than 23 years. He is a Licensed Real Estate Salesperson based in Garden City, where he leads Keller Williams Greater Nassau and works with agents and leadership teams to turn strategy into steady results and better client service.

His career runs through complex markets, distressed properties, shifting buyer expectations and fast changes in technology. Earlier roles in systems integration and facilities management shaped how he approaches process and technology today.

For buyers and sellers, that means a plan built on real data and clear steps. He holds the Seniors Real Estate Specialist (SRES) designation, works across Long Island's South Shore, and has long ties to the South Asian community.

He writes The Key with Bisendra, a weekly newsletter, and speaks about real estate strategy, team performance and the practical use of AI in real estate.

## Left out on purpose
- Office production figures (545 transactions and $425 million in 2025, Q1 2026 numbers). Those are the office's results, not personal sales, so they stay off a buyer and seller facing bio.
- "CEO" and "CAIO". New York limits the titles licensees can use in advertising. Add a title only after your broker confirms it.
