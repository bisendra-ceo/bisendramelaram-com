# What's needed from Bisendra

Each item matches a yellow `[To fill: ...]` box on the site.

## Needed to launch
1. Headshot, at least 800 by 800 pixels (`site/assets/bisendra-melaram.jpg`). A text social card (`og.png`) is in place until then.
2. Done: LinkedIn About section received.
3. Done: seller-first bio with "Team Leader of Keller Williams Greater Nassau" and the 2025 office results credited to the office (see BIO.md). Please approve the short bio. Have your broker OK "Team Leader" in advertising; it always appears next to your license type.
4. Public email address. Cell number only if you want it public.
5. Fair housing notice and KW Greater Nassau SOP links, from your broker.

## Needed to rank
6. Done: LinkedIn, Zillow, Substack and your Google Business Profile are in `SAME_AS`. Add Realtor.com, Homes.com, YouTube and Instagram if you want them.
7. Done: designations and certifications added (SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS). Optional: the year you earned each.
8. Languages you work in with clients, if any besides English.
9. Other areas to name (Garden City, central Nassau, Queens?).
10. How you market listings, how you present pricing to a seller, and how you time a sale with a purchase.
11. Two or three anonymized seller stories, including a downsizing client.
12. Link to the South Asian real estate professional chapter, once it has a public page.
13. Done: the About page links to your Zillow and Google reviews. Reviews are not copied onto the site without permission.

Nothing here is invented: no reviews, sales numbers, awards or press were added.
