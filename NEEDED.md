# What's needed from Bisendra

Each item matches a yellow `[To fill: ...]` box on the site.

## Needed to launch
1. Headshot, at least 800 by 800 pixels (`site/assets/bisendra-melaram.jpg`). Also used as the social share image.
2. Short bio in your words: how you got into real estate, two or three specifics a client would repeat.
3. The leadership title you're allowed to show, cleared with your broker (see SETUP.md compliance note). The site says "leads and develops agents" until then.
4. Public email address. Cell number only if you want it public.
5. Fair housing notice and KW Greater Nassau SOP links, from your broker.

## Needed to rank
6. Profile URLs for `SAME_AS` in `tools/build.py`: Google Business Profile, Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, LinkedIn, Substack (The Key), YouTube, Instagram, Facebook.
7. Other designations to list, with the year earned (the strategy doc mentions AHWD; confirm).
8. Languages you work in with clients, if any besides English.
9. Other areas to name (Garden City, central Nassau, Queens?).
10. How you work with first-time buyers and how you market listings.
11. Two or three anonymized examples of SRES or downsizing clients.
12. Link to the South Asian real estate professional chapter, once it has a public page.
13. Links to your Google and Zillow reviews once they're live. Reviews are not copied onto the site without permission.

Nothing here is invented: no reviews, sales numbers, awards or press were added.
