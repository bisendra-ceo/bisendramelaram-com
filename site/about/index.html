About Bisendra Melaram

Bisendra Melaram is a New York Licensed Real Estate Salesperson based at Keller Williams Greater Nassau, 400 Garden City Plaza in Garden City. What is Bisendra's background?

Bisendra Melaram has worked in New York real estate for more than 23 years. He is a Licensed Real Estate Salesperson based in Garden City, where he leads Keller Williams Greater Nassau and works with agents and leadership teams to turn strategy into steady results and better client service.

His career runs through complex markets, distressed properties, shifting buyer expectations and fast changes in technology. Earlier roles in systems integration and facilities management shaped how he approaches process and technology today.
