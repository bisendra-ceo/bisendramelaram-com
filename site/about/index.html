About Bisendra Melaram

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Bisendra Melaram is the Team Leader of Keller Williams Greater Nassau, 400 Garden City Plaza in Garden City, and a seller's agent on Long Island's South Shore. A New York Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003, he holds the Seller Representative Specialist (SRS) designation and Pricing Strategy Advisor (PSA) certification, and represents buyers too.

How does he lead Keller Williams Greater Nassau?

Bisendra Melaram is the Team Leader of Keller Williams Greater Nassau in Garden City, where he runs the brokerage like a high-performing real estate team with himself at the helm. In 2025 the office closed 545 transactions representing more than $425 million in sales volume, and in the first quarter of 2026 its agents closed 111 units totaling $81.8 million. He works with agents and leadership teams to turn strategy into measurable production and stronger client service.

Office figures as Bisendra reports them on his LinkedIn profile. They are totals for the whole Keller Williams Greater Nassau office, not his personal sales.

What is Bisendra's background?

He is also a seller's agent on Long Island's South Shore and a Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003, with more than 23 years in New York real estate. His career runs through complex markets, distressed properties, shifting buyer expectations and fast changes in technology. Earlier roles in systems integration and facilities management shaped how he approaches process and technology today.

What does that mean for his clients?

For sellers, that means a price built on real closed sales, a house prepared for the questions buyers ask now, and clear steps from listing to closing. He also represents buyers, often clients who are selling one home and buying the next. He holds the SRS, SRES and ABR designations and the PSA, SFR, MRP, AHWD and RSPS certifications and has long ties to the South Asian community.

He writes The Key with Bisendra, a weekly newsletter, and speaks about real estate strategy, team performance and the practical use of AI in real estate.

What credentials does he hold?

A New York real estate license since 2003, plus three designations and five certifications from the National Association of REALTORS family of programs. The seller-side ones come first.

  • New York State Licensed Real Estate Salesperson since 2003
  • Seller Representative Specialist (SRS), designation
  • Pricing Strategy Advisor (PSA), certification
  • Seniors Real Estate Specialist (SRES), designation
  • Accredited Buyer's Representative (ABR), designation
  • Short Sales and Foreclosure Resource (SFR), certification
  • Military Relocation Professional (MRP), certification
  • At Home With Diversity (AHWD), certification
  • Resort and Second-Home Property Specialist (RSPS), certification

Names and categories: National Association of REALTORS acronym guide.
