Buying a home on Long Island's South Shore

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Bisendra Melaram helps buyers find and win homes on Long Island's South Shore. He starts with what changes the price and the monthly cost of a house here: the flood zone, the school district, whether it sits in a village, and the LIRR commute. Then he helps you write an offer that holds up through inspection and closing.

How does buying with Bisendra work?

  1. A first conversation about budget, commute and must-haves.
  2. A written buyer agreement, which New York REALTORS now need before touring.
  3. Preapproval with a lender you choose.
  4. Tours, with a flood zone and school district check on every home you like.
  5. An offer built on recent sales on that street, not a list-price guess.
  6. Inspection, attorney review, contract and closing.

What makes the South Shore different?

Water. Many South Shore neighborhoods sit near bays and canals, and some are in FEMA flood zones that can require flood insurance. Since March 20, 2024, New York sellers can no longer skip the property condition disclosure by giving a $500 credit, and the form now asks about flood zones, flood insurance and past flood claims. Read the South Shore flood zone guide before you bid.

Moving from Queens or Brooklyn?

Valley Stream sits on the Queens border, and the towns east of it follow the LIRR's Babylon and Long Beach branches. Start with the town guides, which list each town's stations, branches and school districts.

What else do people ask?

Do I need a buyer agreement before touring homes on Long Island?

Yes. Since August 2024, agents who are REALTORS must have a written agreement with a buyer before touring a home with them. The agreement spells out what the agent does and how they are paid. Bisendra walks through it with you before your first showing.

What should I check before buying on the South Shore?

Check the FEMA flood zone for the exact address, ask the seller for the flood answers on the New York property condition disclosure, get a flood insurance quote, confirm which school district serves the street, and find out whether the home is in an incorporated village.

Is Long Island a good move from Queens or Brooklyn?

For many buyers it is, if the commute works. The South Shore towns closest to the city sit on the Babylon, Long Beach and Far Rockaway branches of the LIRR, with service to Penn Station and Grand Central Madison. Price, taxes and flood risk vary a lot from one town to the next.
