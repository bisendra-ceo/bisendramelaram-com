How does Bisendra price your home?

From closed sales, not from what neighbors are asking. He looks at similar homes that sold nearby, then adjusts for size, condition, lot, flood zone and school district. The full method is in how to price a home in Nassau County.

How do you prepare the house?

Buyers on the South Shore ask about flood history, permits and the age of the roof and systems. Preparation means answering those questions before a buyer's inspector raises them, then getting the house ready for photos.

  • Repairs an inspector will flag, handled or priced in.
  • Open permits and certificates of occupancy checked with the town or village.
  • The property condition disclosure, flood answers included, ready before listing.
  • Decluttering and staging, room by room.

How do you compare and negotiate offers?

An offer is more than a price. Compare the financing and down payment, the inspection and mortgage contingencies, whether the buyer has a home to sell first, the closing date, and any credits requested. A higher price with a weak contingency can be the worse offer.

What happens between contract and closing?

On Long Island, the attorneys draw up the contract once the offer is accepted. Then come the buyer's inspection, appraisal and mortgage commitment, the title search, and a final walk-through before closing. At closing, your mortgage payoff, the New York transfer tax, the commission and any tax adjustments come out of the proceeds. See what it costs to sell.

What else do people ask?

Who does Bisendra represent when I list with him?

You, the seller. New York requires agents to explain in writing, on the state's agency disclosure form, who they represent. As your listing agent he owes his duties to you, not to the buyer.

What should I gather before the first meeting?

Your recent tax bills, your deed and survey if you have them, any flood insurance policy and elevation certificate, permits and certificates of occupancy for past work, and a list of repairs you know about.

How long does it take to sell a home in Nassau County?

In OneKey MLS figures for Nassau County single-family homes, the 2026 median days on market ran from 38 days in the summer to 58 days in March. Closing usually follows the contract by several more weeks, once the buyer's financing and attorney review are done.

Does Bisendra handle the property condition disclosure?

He helps you prepare for it. The form is the seller's to complete and sign, and since March 20, 2024, most New York sellers must deliver it. It now includes flood questions, so he works through those answers with you before the home goes on the market.
