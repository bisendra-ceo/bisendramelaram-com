When is the best time to list a home on the South Shore?

By Bisendra Melaram, SRS, PSA, SRES, ABR, SFR, MRP, AHWD, RSPS, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson. Last updated November 8, 2026.

In 2026, Nassau County single-family homes sold fastest and closest to their asking price from June through August, when median days on market fell to 38 to 42 and sellers received 100.5 to 101.3 percent of original list price, per OneKey MLS. New listings peaked in April, and 2025 followed the same pattern.

What do the 2025 and 2026 numbers show?

Across both years, homes that closed in late spring and summer sold faster and for a larger share of their original price than homes that closed in winter. Closed sales peaked in July and August. New listings were highest in April and May, which means more competition for sellers listing then.

Is there an advantage to listing in winter?

Less competition. At the end of January 2026 there were 1,497 single-family homes for sale in Nassau County, compared with 2,327 at the end of June. The tradeoff in 2026 was a longer median time to sell, 55 days in January, and a lower share of the original price, 97.2 percent.

Does the month matter more than the price?

No. A well-priced, well-prepared home sells in any month, and an overpriced one sits in May. Use the season to plan your timing, then price from closed sales and prepare the house. See how to price a Nassau County home.

How should South Shore sellers plan around the calendar?

  • Give yourself time to prepare. Repairs, permits and the disclosure form, including its flood answers, take weeks, not days.
  • Know your tax dates. Town of Hempstead school taxes are due in halves on October 1 and April 1, and general taxes on January 1 and July 1, and they are adjusted at closing.
  • Line up your next move before you list, especially if you are buying in the same season everyone else is.

What else do people ask?

What is the best month to sell a house in Nassau County?

In 2025 and 2026 OneKey MLS data, homes that closed in June, July and August sold fastest and for the highest share of their original list price. Because closings follow contracts by weeks, that points to listing in spring.

Are there fewer buyers in winter on Long Island?

OneKey's county figures show fewer closed sales and fewer homes for sale in January and February. Fewer listings means less competition for the sellers who do list.

Do these numbers apply to my town?

They are countywide figures for Nassau single-family homes. Town patterns can differ, so ask for the closed sales in your own town and section.
