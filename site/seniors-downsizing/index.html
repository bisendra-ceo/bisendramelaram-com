Downsizing on Long Island with an SRES agent

By Bisendra Melaram, SRES, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Bisendra Melaram holds the Seniors Real Estate Specialist (SRES) designation and helps Long Island homeowners 50 and older sell the family home and plan the next one. That can mean a smaller home nearby, a home near children, or a move off Long Island. He works at your pace and with your family, attorney and financial adviser.

What does an SRES agent do differently?

An SRES agent is trained on the questions that come with a later-life move: timing a sale with a purchase, sorting a house full of belongings, working with adult children, and knowing when to bring in an attorney or tax adviser. How do you sell a home you have lived in for decades?

One room at a time. Start with a plan for what stays, what goes to family and what gets sold or donated. Then fix the few things a buyer's inspector will flag, and price from what similar homes nearby have sold for.

What about the family?

Adult children often help, and sometimes live far away. Bisendra keeps everyone you choose in the loop, and takes direction from you.

Common questions

What is an SRES?

SRES stands for Seniors Real Estate Specialist, a designation from the National Association of REALTORS for agents trained to help clients 50 and older buy, sell or relocate. Bisendra Melaram holds the SRES designation.

When is the right time to downsize?

When the house costs more in money, time or worry than it gives back. Common signs are stairs that have become hard, rooms nobody uses, and taxes or upkeep that strain a fixed income. The right time is your call, and a good agent will not rush it.

Can I buy my next home before I sell this one?

Sometimes. It depends on your savings, your equity and what a lender will approve. Talk to a lender and your attorney early, and ask Bisendra how sale and purchase dates can be lined up.
